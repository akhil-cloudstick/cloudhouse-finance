KYC Vendor Comparison — PAN & Aadhaar Verification API

Ambika Gold Finance — Loan Operations — 13 Aug 2026

Recommendation: Setu, for both PAN and Aadhaar. It's the only vendor with real published per-call pricing instead of a sales call, its PAN check is a single-field API call, and it already matches the Customer.PAN / AadhaarEncrypted fields in the codebase. The DigiLocker consent step on Aadhaar is not a Setu-specific limitation — UIDAI retired raw-OTP e-KYC for every private aggregator, so switching vendors does not remove that step.

On pricing: Setu is the only vendor in this list that publishes real per-call rupee pricing. Everyone else keeps pricing behind a sales call — where a rupee figure could be found at all below, it is a third-party estimate or a price for a different/bundled service, not that vendor's own published rate, and is marked as such.
